Privacy

Overview

Gridcoil is a standalone arcade game. It does not require a first-party account and does not operate a developer-owned analytics or gameplay backend.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, local score history, best score, rounds played, pending Game Center submissions, and advertising cadence are stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. Gridcoil does not send that local history to a developer-owned server.

Game Center

If you choose to use Game Center, Apple processes your player identity and submitted high score under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Game Center is optional; the game and local history work while signed out.

Advertising and consent

Gridcoil uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform when advertising is enabled in a production build. Google may process device, app, consent, diagnostic, and advertising information according to the ad configuration and your region. The app requests advertising only when the consent SDK reports that requests are permitted, and offers privacy options when required.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses advertising data.

Your choices

  • You may play without Game Center.
  • You may use the in-app privacy options when shown.
  • You may remove locally stored Gridcoil data by deleting the app.
  • System-level advertising and Game Center choices remain available in iOS Settings.

Contact

For a Gridcoil privacy or support question, contact the developer through the public profile and do not include passwords or private account information.
